# Lens (Mirantis Lens K8S IDE / Lens Desktop) vs Kubebay inventory F01-F40

Researcher: desk research, 2026-11-12. All "evidence" dates are 2026-11-12.
Scope note: the brief said F01-F40, but inventory.md runs to F78; only F01-F40 are covered here.
Method caveat: WebFetch uses a small summariser; many Lens docs pages are short and describe tables and menus, not every panel section. So "page does not mention X" is weak evidence and is mostly reported as UNCLEAR, not NOT-DOCUMENTED. The sitemap (https://docs.lenshq.io/sitemap.xml) was fetched and read in full: there is no page named for timeline, saved views, node-shell, theme, VPA recommender, pod-resize, finalizers, or force-delete.

## Weakest findings
1. F13 (NOT-DOCUMENTED, info button on every page): absence evidence is only docs and release notes; I could not inspect the app, and a tooltip/help button may exist undocumented. Do not publish "Lens lacks this".
2. F24/F25 (NOT-DOCUMENTED rollout tab and Timeline): the docs never itemise details-panel tabs, so the pages I checked would not mention these even if present. Treat as UNCLEAR for public claims; the Events tab on every detail panel does exist (release 2026.5.18).
3. F34/F37 (NOT-DOCUMENTED pod resize, force delete/finalizers): same limitation; the pod menu is documented, but the details panel and edit flow are not. Finalizers can be removed by editing YAML in any editor, so a "no" claim would be misleading.
4. F30/F38/F01/F07 (UNCLEAR): docs describe tables and menus, not panel contents; the WebFetch summariser returned truncated pages. They need a look at the real app, not the docs.
5. F03 (isolated kubeconfig mode): I saw Kubeconfig Syncs and the ~/.kube auto-detect, but could not establish whether the default can be removed (asked on two pages). Also F08 (palette scope) rests on one summariser answer.

Also unresolved across all rows: whether the free Personal plan requires periodic online sign-in/telemetry. FAQ says a Lens ID is needed to activate; privacy page said "Lens ID, Lens K8S IDE Kubernetes, and Lens Teamwork are the only features requiring an internet connection" (summariser paraphrase of https://docs.lenshq.io/security-and-compliance/privacy/). Relevant to any comparison claim about F75-style "no account", although that is outside F01-F40.
